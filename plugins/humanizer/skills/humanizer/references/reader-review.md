# Reader review

Use after the initial draft when structure, reader context or navigation needs review, or during an explicitly requested read-only audit. Apply only the relevant checks within the requested scope and intensity. These are editorial questions, not a required document template, another candidate-generation stage or an authorship test. The complete original and [verification contract](verification.md) remain authoritative.

## Read the artifact without the conversation

Identify what this reader needs to understand or do from the request and supplied material. A newsletter, reference page and personal message need different framing; none requires a new call to action by default.

For an artifact meant to stand alone, check references such as “that option,” unexplained abbreviations and replies to an invisible question. Resolve them only when the supplied context identifies the referent. Do not invent a definition, background fact or missing decision. Conversational language remains appropriate in an actual reply or dialogue.

**Control:** If the source defines “offline mode,” a detached paragraph can name that mode instead of “the option above.” If no option is identified, flag the missing context outside the rewrite rather than choosing one.

## Follow the progression and entry points

Follow the opening sentences together, in document order. Check topic continuity, time jumps, prerequisites and whether each paragraph advances the intended explanation. Interchangeable sections can be legitimate in a catalogue; reorderability alone is not a defect. Repair a concrete gap without adding an unsupported cause or forcing every paragraph to depend on the previous one.

Inspect a section as someone arriving directly at its heading, procedure step, summary or notice. The reader must still find the relevant subject, conditions and next step when those are supplied. Reordering can require repairing an editable “below” reference; it does not authorize changing protected reference targets.

If a heading is a known link target, keep its existing anchor stable, retaining its wording when necessary. Automatic fragment anchors can change with heading text. Protected IDs, link targets and citation markers stay exact unless an explicit exception authorizes coordinated changes. Check known links after an authorized heading change; do not claim unseen inbound links were checked.

## Distinguish repetition from repeated access

Ask what each occurrence does for its reader before deleting it. A summary may orient someone who skips the body; a prerequisite may belong beside each independently usable procedure. Retain a short repeated warning when removing it would make one entry point incomplete. Repeated wording, equal section lengths and parallel instructions are not defects by themselves.

**Legitimate use:** “Disconnect power before opening the cover” can belong in both a safety overview and the cover-removal procedure. Its second occurrence is not automatically empty recap.

## Preserve list logic and relationships

Before changing prose into bullets or a table, identify cumulative requirements, alternatives, exceptions, shared conditions and the hierarchy of wholes and parts. Keep a shared lead-in attached to every governed item. Preserve ordered dependencies and the meaning of all, any, only, unless and their language-specific equivalents.

**Control:** “Approval requires both a signed form and a current permit” must not become a choice between them. A system and its two components are not three equivalent alternatives.

Matching numbers or retaining every noun does not prove fidelity. “East: 12 visits from 5 people; West: 5 visits from 12 people” changes meaning if the visits and people are swapped, although the token inventory stays the same. Recheck the entity, quantity, denominator, time and qualification together.

Keep each citation attached to the proposition it supports. Joining sentences must not make one citation appear to support an additional claim; splitting must not leave the supported claim unattributed. Preserve identifiers and technical terms rather than cycling synonyms.

## Surface missing substance honestly

Check whether the supplied material answers its stated purpose. If a notice asks readers to submit a form but omits the submission method needed to act, identify that omission outside the copy. Do not require a deadline or call to action where the task does not need one. Never fabricate a missing step, contact, number, evidence or promise. Make supported repairs within the existing revision budget; a valid unchanged passage and an explicit unresolved gap remain acceptable outcomes.
