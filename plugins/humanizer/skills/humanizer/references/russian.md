# Russian editorial guidance

Use for editable Russian prose, including Russian segments in mixed text. These are contextual editing choices, not Russian AI markers or a replacement dictionary. Apply the entry point's scope and integrity rules; English word lists, contractions and rhythm targets do not transfer automatically.

## Draft from the intended meaning

For a full rewrite, identify who does what, to which object, under what conditions and in what order, then write the connected passage in natural Russian. Russian can leave a subject implicit or use an impersonal construction naturally. Do not add «мы», «команда» or another actor merely to make a verb active. Keep a source's formal or technical register where it serves the reader.

Review these constructions when they obscure the point:

| Construction | Useful edit and boundary |
|--------------|-------------------------|
| Bureaucratic nominalizations | «Команда осуществляет проверку заявлений» → «Команда проверяет заявления». The actor and action are supplied. Keep an established process name such as «проверка целостности», rather than changing terminology for variety. |
| Impersonal/passive padding | «Проверка заявлений производится только в рабочие дни» → «Заявления проверяют только в рабочие дни». No checker is invented. «Заявление отклонено» may be the right concise status; active voice is optional. |
| Stacked genitives | Unpack relationships only when clear from context: «Команда определяет срок хранения копий отчётов» → «Команда определяет, как долго хранить копии отчётов». Keep «копии отчётов», rather than silently changing the object to the reports themselves. A compact technical phrase can remain. |
| English calques and collocation | «Команда сделает решение о запуске после проверки» → «Команда примет решение о запуске после проверки». Prefer an idiomatic verb/object pair without changing the action. Do not replace established terms such as «робастная регрессия» or technical «реализация» merely because they resemble English. |
| Repeated connective phrases | Remove «Кроме того» or «Таким образом» only where the relationship remains clear. Keep «Однако» for an actual contrast and «поэтому» for a supplied consequence; do not introduce causality to make a transition smoother. |
| Generic framing | «Важно отметить, что срок ожидания — 30 секунд» → «Срок ожидания — 30 секунд» when the introduction adds no useful emphasis. A warning such as «Не отключайте питание во время обновления» remains substantive even after an empty introduction is cut. |

Do not flatten native word order into a fixed subject–verb–object pattern. Information already known can precede a new or emphasized fact. Repeating a noun may be clearer than «он», «она» or «это» when several referents are possible. Keep deliberate repetition, parallel requirements and useful headings.

## Check grammatical seams after editing

After moving, combining or replacing clauses, read the resulting Russian sentence as a whole:

- **Case, government and agreement:** Check the case required by a changed verb or preposition, agreement of subject/predicate and noun/modifier, and coordinated forms. «Согласно отчёту» and «по данным отдела» retain their own grammar; do not splice fragments from different constructions.
- **Aspect and time:** Preserve a recurring action versus a completed result. «Проверяют» does not become «проверили», and «может сократить» does not become «сократил». Keep «уже», «пока», «ещё», «до» and «после» where they define the claim or procedure.
- **Modality and negation:** Distinguish «нужно», «можно», «может», «не должен» and «не обязан». «Не проверено» does not mean «не работает»; «может помочь» does not mean «помогает». Keep «только» attached to what it restricts.
- **Participial and adverbial attachment:** Keep a participle attached to its intended noun and a деепричастие attached to the actor performing both actions. «Проверив записи, команда удалила дубликаты» is supported only when the team is the supplied actor for both actions. If the actor is unspecified, keep the sequence without assigning it: «После проверки записей дубликаты удалили».
- **Referents and collocations:** Recheck ambiguous pronouns, modifiers, prepositional phrases and natural word combinations. A shorter synonym can change a technical object, an obligation or the extent of a claim.
- **Rank and extent:** «Прежде всего» can rank a reason; it is not automatically introductory filler. «Помогает следить за историей» covers more than «помогает войти в историю». Preserve the extent of the original judgment.
- **Explicit roles and clause relationships:** «Трумана играет Джим Керри» supplies an actor–role relation; mentioning only «игра Керри» can lose it. Keep a clear relation even when a reader might know the film. «Условность делает сатиру яснее» must not become an added claim that some other scene «ясно показывает» a point.
- **Contrast and emphasis:** «А», «но» and «зато» do different work; do not swap them to vary transitions. Particles such as «даже», «ведь» and «всё-таки» can carry expectation, explanation or concession. Delete them only when their contribution is genuinely redundant in context. Keep a contextually recoverable subject recoverable, but do not demand an actor's identity when the source leaves it unspecified; distinguish that omission from a change in agency.

«Не пытается объяснить» describes intent; «не объясняет» alone may lose that distinction. Assess the whole passage under the shared [verification contract](verification.md), which supplies the semantic decision standard.

For example, «По данным отдела, новый порядок может сократить ожидание только в рабочие дни; сельские отделения не участвовали в проверке» retains attribution, possibility, the weekday restriction and the excluded population. Do not turn it into «Новый порядок сокращает ожидание» or move «только» so that it restricts a different action.

## Preserve address, spelling and evidence

- **Address and intent:** Keep the established «ты/вы», level of formality and force of a request unless the user asks to change them. «Пожалуйста, загрузите отчёт» can become «Загрузите, пожалуйста, отчёт», not an unrequested «Загрузи отчёт». A quoted speaker can use «ты» inside an otherwise formal document; that is not a consistency error.
- **ё/е conventions:** Follow the supplied text or applicable style guidance rather than globally inserting/removing «ё». Both «ещё не готово» and the established spelling «еще не готово» can remain. Correct a clear spelling or grammatical error in editable prose when context establishes the meaning; do not guess between meanings such as «все/всё». Keep the exact spelling of official names, quotations and identifiers, including «Семён» versus «Семен».
- **Work status:** Distinguish preparation, handoff, testing, approval and release. «Исправление подготовили и передали на проверку» does not mean «Исправление проверили и выпустили». Conversely, a source's explicit «Проверку завершили» remains a completed check; do not weaken it to a plan for extra caution.
- **Evidence and speaker:** «В документации указано, что функция доступна; я её пока не проверял» must retain the documentation claim and the absence of the author's verification. «Я заметил» is an observation, «я предполагаю» a hypothesis, and «по словам коллеги» an attributed report. Do not turn one into another or present a private observation as a general measured result.
- **Unresolved details:** In «В письме — 12 мая, в заявке — 14 мая; дата не подтверждена», keep both dates attached to their sources unless an explicit author correction resolves them. Preserve placeholders such as «до [дата]» and meaningful unfinished fragments; flag the missing detail outside the rewrite rather than choosing a date or completing the claim by guesswork.

## Legitimate constructions to keep

- «Москва — столица России» and «Время ожидания — 30 секунд» use grammatical dashes. Keep dialogue punctuation and a dash that clarifies a genuine contrast or explanation; do not substitute a hyphen to reduce a count.
- «Заявления проверяют только в рабочие дни; проверяющий не указан» can remain impersonal and neutral. The missing actor is not a stylistic defect to fill.
- «Для заявления нужны имя, адрес и подпись» is a complete three-item requirement. Its regularity is useful.
- «По данным отдела, новый порядок может сократить ожидание» retains both attribution and uncertainty. Do not strip either to sound more decisive.
- Formal and technical expressions such as «настоящий договор», «срок исковой давности» and «робастная регрессия» may be precise in their intended contexts. Simplify surrounding prose when useful; do not casually rewrite the terms.

In mixed text, edit the Russian segments under this guidance and leave protected text in its original language. An agent's Russian review remains an editorial assessment, not native-human approval or measured detector performance.
