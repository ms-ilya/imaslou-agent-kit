# Source preservation and compact preparation

Read the complete original and actual request before drafting. They remain authoritative throughout the run. A brief, anchor or byte map records an interpretation or location; none proves that every claim has been understood.

## Meaning and expression

Preserve claims, opinions, intent, uncertainty, degree, actors, attribution, quantities, causality, conditions, exceptions and scope. Include speech acts, ranking, chronology, AND/OR membership and cross-paragraph references. A request is not an order; an estimate is not a promise; an attempt is not an outcome. Preserve meaningful courtesies and deliberate rhetoric. Do not add facts, experience, opinions or unsupported caution.

Only content-free filler, genuinely redundant restatements and unwanted chatbot wrappers may disappear without an abridgment request. Before deleting a passage, check for unique qualifications, attribution, relationships or useful reader context, including in the final paragraph. Lack of a citation does not authorize removing a source claim.

Ordinary wording, sentence frames, paragraph boundaries and grouping may change. Equivalent expression can preserve a qualifier without repeating its token. A retained token attached to the wrong actor or proposition still fails. Exact locks apply to protected material, technically necessary or deliberately distinctive wording and explicit user-kept spans, not every meaning-bearing word.

Apply explicit user corrections. Otherwise preserve contradictory accounts with their attribution, placeholders, unresolved ambiguity and meaningful unfinished fragments; do not choose an unsupported resolution. Flag a material source problem outside the rewrite and complete safe edits that do not depend on it.

## Before drafting

Freeze a compact brief: reader (or unknown), genre, register, purpose, languages, intensity and actual request. Source identity and stance stay fixed; source habits need not dictate sentence structure. Relevant samples supply expression evidence, never facts, persona or experience. For supplied samples or full treatment, consult the applicable parts of [voice guidance](voice-archetypes.md); read [Russian guidance](russian.md) for Russian segments.

Choose a few source-specific objectives with locations, reader impact and intended action. An explicit rewrite allows recomposition of grammatical prose without a changed-word quota. If no change is justified, record the reason instead; a plan without objectives permits only original bytes. Unchanged output cannot be claimed as improved.

For captured files, prepare the complete source-unit partition, section/relationship links and exact protected/out-of-scope spans before drafting. Read every unit and its surrounding relationships. Include separators and all bytes outside scope. The read-only `stages.py index` command supplies physical block offsets, not meaning, scope or protection decisions. The [stage guide](stages.md) contains the schemas and commands.

Record selective source-only questions for fragile relationships using exact byte ranges. For example, ask what a limiting clause governs, which actor reports a claim, or whether the source describes intent or outcome. Use multiple ranges for a cross-unit relationship. The helper derives quotations from captured bytes; do not write a comprehensive paraphrase or preparatory answer for every unit. Empty anchors are valid. Dense passages may need many anchors; there is no count or size target. Selecting reminders never replaces reading the full source.

Classify structural recomposition, dense relationships, mixed languages and delicate protected boundaries before drafting. These risks, multiple languages, more than 2000 source words or an explicit request require an independent attempt. Actual runtime/model/settings/watermark information stays unknown when unobserved.

## Draft and trace

Keep the entire source beside the brief. Draft one connected passage within the frozen meaning and scope; anchors are reminders, not the factual source. Choose sentence and paragraph boundaries for the reader. Neutral stance permits source-compatible imagery, emphasis and idiomatic phrasing without added opinions or a persona.

After drafting, compute candidate byte ranges and supply source-unit associations plus the exact occurrence coordinates of fixed spans. Every candidate unit and every source unit must be accounted for. Splitting, merging and reordering are allowed. A genuinely redundant source passage maps to its surviving expression; that judgment still needs semantic review. Never reuse old offsets after rephrasing or select the first repeated literal substring without checking which occurrence it is.

Record the exact candidate and map together under the reserved ticket. The keeper retains malformed maps and failed candidates, consumes the attempt and blocks mechanically invalid output. There is no later mutable bookkeeping stage. Follow the [verification contract](verification.md) for complete source → candidate and candidate → source review before repair.

The original overrides a missing or wrong anchor. Record an annotation defect in review evidence rather than weakening the frozen plan. If scope/partition errors prevent valid mechanical checks, retain the source and disclose the preparation failure; do not restart to gain attempts. Short targeted inline edits may keep preparation in context and disclose unavailable byte checks without inventing offsets or coverage.
