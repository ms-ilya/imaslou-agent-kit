# Evidence and limits

Humanizer is an editorial workflow for clear, natural prose that preserves the supplied meaning. Its pattern lists are contextual review prompts, not an authorship test. No reliable detector advantage or universal semantic-fidelity guarantee has been established for this plugin.

## Preservation and verification

Follow the [source-preservation procedure](preservation.md) and shared [verification contract](verification.md). Review the complete original in both directions, including qualifications, actors, attribution, scope and logical relationships. Repair supported fidelity failures before optional style changes within the existing revision limits.

Selective exact-range questions help locate fragile relationships; they are not an exhaustive meaning inventory. Literal matches do not prove that relationships survived. Local helpers check artifact identity, report structure and bounded transitions. They cannot establish semantic truth. Independent agent review is a separate assessment, not native-human validation or an objective quality measure.

## Detector and watermark limits

- Requested editorial scores are descriptive and never gate acceptance. They are not calibrated authorship probabilities or detector scores.
- A raw AI source already classified as human provides no label-level rewriting gain. Keep native scores, labels, eligibility and human-control outcomes separate; do not average unlike provider scales.
- Missing, unsupported, gated or failed checks are unavailable coverage, never zero AI or a measured pass. Development feedback is not untouched evaluation.
- English research does not establish Russian or other-language performance. Genre, length, editing, model changes and human-reference selection limit transfer.
- Unicode observations are not AI signatures. Preserve legitimate and ambiguous characters, protected spans and token boundaries. Read-only inspection does not authorize normalization or cleanup.
- Generic classifiers and keyed statistical watermarks are different mechanisms. Character inspection cannot certify removal of a private vendor watermark.

No detector call, additional rewrite engine, model installation or final mutating cleaner is part of ordinary delivery. Use only actually obtained, eligible feedback under the existing contract; do not predict percentages or promise detector passage.

## Validation scope

Local regression tests exercise mechanics and packaging. Limited agent-reviewed examples and detector diagnostics do not establish native-human preference, production consistency or a general detector-evasion result. Historical development reports are maintainer records and are not required drafting inputs.

Keep byte hygiene, editorial quality, generic detector results and keyed watermark verification distinct. Record observed generation/watermark settings, or `unknown` when unavailable.
